## Category and product role

Seamwise is an architecture-aware, model-agnostic intent-to-task compiler: the
missing middle between an accepted delivery outcome and trustworthy,
proof-bearing work.

It is not a backlog generator, general execution runtime, or generic AI
orchestrator. It discovers the right work and its safe order while preserving
the system model that makes each task legitimate.

## Canonical product chain

```text
Delivery Intent + evidence
  -> accepted seams
  -> exactly one owning swimlane per seam
  -> observable capability legs
  -> dependency- and contention-aware semantic task graph
  -> validated, unsealed Task-Specs
```

Task-Spec defines a known atomic job. Seamwise discovers which jobs are
required, their boundaries, dependencies, and safe order. Converge remains the
external system for authorization, execution, evidence, settlement, and
learning.

## Primary audience

- Staff and principal engineers who must preserve architecture while delegating
  implementation.
- Platform and solution architects turning cross-system intent into coherent
  delivery boundaries.
- Technical leads operating multi-agent engineering workflows.
- Tool builders who need deterministic, inspectable artifacts between planning
  and execution.

The identity should work for an expert audience without becoming cold,
militaristic, or ceremonially complex.

## Functional promise

Turn one observable delivery outcome plus evidence into the right,
dependency-safe, independently provable Task-Spec DAG without erasing
architecture, ownership, lineage, or authority boundaries.

## Emotional promise

Calm confidence before delegation.

The user should feel that complexity has been made legible, the dangerous
ambiguities have names, and the work can move without someone reconstructing
the architecture from a flat ticket list.

## Differentiating idea

**Meaning-preserving work lowering.**

Other systems split a request into activities. Seamwise finds defensible system
joints, gives each joint one coherent owner, stages observable capability
states, and carries the reason and proof for every resulting task.

## Product action

1. Find and refute candidate joints using evidence.
2. Preserve each accepted boundary as one owning swimlane.
3. Stage capability states rather than activity labels.
4. Compile real dependencies, contention, and integration proof.
5. Materialize validated Task-Specs while leaving authority explicit and
   unsealed.

The identity should suggest discernment and controlled transformation, not
instantaneous automation.

## Trust model

- Evidence, hashes, lineage, schemas, and runtime results support claims.
- Missing evidence, ownership, decisions, review, or proof closes the gate.
- Models may research, propose, attack, or author; they are never canonical
  sources of truth.
- Review, validation, preflight, sealing, execution, and acceptance are distinct
  transitions.
- Ordinary compilation never auto-approves or auto-seals work.
- Telemetry observes; it does not authorize.

Visually, this means visible boundaries, deliberate gaps, stable geometry,
strong one-color behavior, and no magical glow standing in for trust.

## Desired personality

- Architectural, not decorative.
- Exact, not brittle.
- Calm, not passive.
- Discerning, not bureaucratic.
- Technical, not cyberpunk.
- Premium through reduction, not gloss.
- Confident enough to stop.

## Symbolic territory

Promising metaphors include:

- a datum joint that makes a defensible boundary visible;
- an architectural weave that coordinates owned capability legs;
- a proof stair that preserves lineage through ordered states;
- topology, load paths, apertures, and interlocking responsibility;
- a controlled transformation from intent to independently provable work.

Round 01 develops the first three as genuinely different strategic directions.

## Visual territory to avoid

- Literal needles, thread spools, stitches, fabric, or craft-store imagery.
- Generic node graphs, circuit traces, Git branches, arrows, and flowchart
  icons.
- Random sparkles, magic wands, or robot imagery.
- Generic purple-blue AI gradients and neon glow as the primary idea.
- An ordinary `S` in a hexagon, shield, circle, or app-tile container.
- Infinity loops, startup blobs, unexplained folds, or meaningless motion.
- Geometry, wordmarks, layouts, or palettes copied from Semattice or Converge.
- Marks that depend on gradients, texture, animation, or large scale.

## Round 01 success criteria

An advancing direction must:

- have an ownable silhouette with a truthful metaphor;
- remain identifiable at 16, 24, and 32 px;
- work in one color and in reverse;
- support an icon and horizontal wordmark;
- remain distinct from the current tracked mark and the quality references;
- avoid implying execution, settlement, or automatic approval;
- be simple enough to rebuild as controlled SVG geometry after approval.
